# Gitd edge patent screen

This is technical screening, not legal advice or patent clearance.

Patent review: **TRIGGERED**. QUIC v2 and bounded HTTP/3 remain under the
Canada-only **CONDITIONAL_PROCEED** entries in `docs/patents/REVIEW_LOG.md`.
Conventional validation, escaping, exact-generation cache keys, quotas, and
LRU bookkeeping add no separately identified mechanism.

General Zstandard compressed-block encoding and any new cache selection or
single-flight mechanism require claim-limitation searching and authoritative
CIPO verification before implementation or release. They remain open; this
document makes no non-infringement conclusion.
