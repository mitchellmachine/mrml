MRML respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information handled through mrml.ca and git.mrml.ca (collectively, the “Services”).

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information may be collected when you visit the Services, create or use a Forgejo account, contribute to repositories, or otherwise interact with the Services, and how that information is used, protected, retained, and disclosed.

1. Who operates the Services

The Services are operated by the administrator of MRML in Canada.

For purposes of applicable Canadian privacy legislation, the Privacy Officer is responsible for the protection of personal information under our control and for responding to privacy questions, requests, and complaints.

Privacy Officer: Mitchell Renouf

Email: mitchellrenouf@mrml.ca

Privacy-related requests may be sent to the email address above.

2. Information we collect

We seek to collect only information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and maintain the Services.

Depending on how you use the Services, information collected may include:

Account information

If you create a Forgejo account, information may include:

  • your username;
  • your email address;
  • your display name, if provided;
  • your profile information, if provided;
  • account preferences and settings; and
  • authentication and security information associated with your account.

Passwords are not intended to be stored in readable form. Authentication credentials are processed and stored using the security mechanisms provided by Forgejo.

Repository and contribution information

When you use Forgejo, information you intentionally provide may become associated with your account or repositories, including:

  • Git commit names and email addresses;
  • commits and commit metadata;
  • repository content;
  • issues;
  • pull requests;
  • comments;
  • releases;
  • attachments;
  • wiki content; and
  • other contributions.

Information published in a public repository should be considered public.

Git is a distributed version-control system. Information contained in Git history may be copied to other systems when a repository is cloned, mirrored, forked, or otherwise distributed. Consequently, removing information from the MRML Forgejo server does not necessarily remove copies that have already been distributed to others.

Technical and security information

When you connect to the Services, the server and supporting infrastructure may process technical information such as:

  • IP addresses;
  • request dates and times;
  • requested URLs or resources;
  • HTTP request information;
  • browser or client information;
  • authentication events;
  • errors;
  • security events; and
  • other server or application log information.

This information is used primarily to operate, troubleshoot, maintain, and protect the Services.

3. Why we collect and use information

Personal information may be used for purposes including:

  • creating and maintaining Forgejo accounts;
  • authenticating users;
  • providing Git repository hosting;
  • displaying contributions and repository activity;
  • processing issues, pull requests, comments, and other collaboration features;
  • communicating service-related information;
  • detecting and preventing spam, abuse, unauthorized access, and attacks;
  • diagnosing technical problems;
  • maintaining the reliability and security of the Services;
  • creating backups and recovering from failures;
  • responding to privacy, security, or support requests; and
  • complying with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to operating the Services unless we have an appropriate legal basis or obtain any consent required by applicable law.

4. Public information

Forgejo is a collaborative source-code hosting platform.

Information that you intentionally publish through a public account, repository, issue, pull request, comment, release, or other public feature may be accessible to anyone on the Internet.

This can include your username, profile information, repository contributions, commit metadata, and any personal information you choose to include in public content.

You should avoid including personal information in public repositories or Git history unless you intend for that information to become public.

5. Cookies

Forgejo may use cookies that are necessary for functionality such as:

  • maintaining authenticated sessions;
  • account security;
  • remembering certain preferences; and
  • protecting requests against security threats.

We do not use cookies for third-party behavioural advertising.

If analytics, advertising, tracking, or other non-essential technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated and any consent required by applicable law will be obtained.

6. Tracking and advertising

The Services are not intended to use third-party behavioural advertising or sell personal information for advertising purposes.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

If these practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated before the new practices are implemented where required by law.

7. Hosting and service providers

The Services are self-hosted using infrastructure located with third-party hosting and network providers.

Those providers may process limited technical information as necessary to provide infrastructure, networking, security, or related services.

Personal information will only be disclosed to service providers to the extent reasonably necessary to provide those services, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Where personal information may be communicated or stored outside Québec or Canada, we will take the measures required by applicable privacy law before such communication occurs.

8. Disclosure of personal information

We do not sell personal information.

Personal information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary:

  • to service providers supporting the operation or security of the Services;
  • with your consent;
  • to investigate abuse, fraud, or security incidents;
  • to protect the rights, safety, integrity, or security of users, the Services, or others;
  • to comply with a valid legal requirement; or
  • where otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

Where legally permitted, we seek to limit disclosures to information reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose.

9. Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected or as required by law.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information.

For example:

  • active account information may be retained while an account remains active;
  • repository and contribution information may remain as part of Git history;
  • security and server logs may be retained temporarily for security, troubleshooting, and operational purposes;
  • information may remain temporarily in backups after deletion from active systems; and
  • certain information may be retained when necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or address security incidents.

When personal information is no longer required, it will be deleted, destroyed, or anonymized as appropriate and as required by applicable law.

10. Backups

The Services may maintain backups for disaster recovery, data integrity, and operational continuity.

Because backups represent historical copies of system data, information deleted from an active system may remain in a backup until that backup expires or is securely replaced or destroyed according to the applicable retention schedule.

Backups are not intended to be used as active records after information has been deleted except where restoration is necessary for legitimate recovery purposes.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information under our control.

Measures may include:

  • encrypted HTTPS connections;
  • access controls;
  • authentication controls;
  • network and firewall protections;
  • software updates and security patches;
  • restricted administrative access;
  • backups;
  • logging and monitoring for security purposes; and
  • other measures intended to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.

No Internet-connected service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Privacy incidents

If a security or privacy incident involving personal information occurs, we will investigate the incident and take reasonable steps to contain and mitigate it.

Where required by applicable Canadian or Québec privacy legislation, we will notify affected individuals and the appropriate privacy authority and maintain records concerning the incident.

13. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law and any applicable exceptions, you may have rights concerning personal information we hold about you.

These may include the right to:

  • request access to your personal information;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information;
  • withdraw consent where processing depends on consent, subject to legal or contractual restrictions;
  • ask questions about how your personal information is collected, used, retained, or disclosed;
  • obtain information about applicable categories of persons with access to your information;
  • request deletion or cessation of dissemination in circumstances provided by applicable law; and
  • make a complaint regarding our handling of your personal information.

Requests can be made to the Privacy Officer at mitchellrenouf@mrml.ca.

We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

Certain requests may be limited where required or permitted by law or where the technical nature of Git and distributed repositories prevents us from controlling copies held by third parties.

14. Account deletion

You may request deletion of your Forgejo account where permitted by applicable law and the functionality of the Services.

Deleting an account does not necessarily remove information that forms part of public Git history or content that has already been copied, cloned, forked, mirrored, archived, or otherwise distributed by other users or systems.

Where appropriate, account information may instead be anonymized or disassociated from an account.

15. Children's privacy

The Services are intended primarily for software development and source-code collaboration and are not specifically directed at children.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children beyond information necessary to provide the Services.

If you believe that personal information relating to a child has been collected inappropriately, contact the Privacy Officer.

16. Automated decisions and profiling

We do not use personal information to make decisions about individuals based exclusively on automated processing, and we do not intentionally use the Services to profile individuals for advertising or behavioural marketing purposes.

If this changes, users will be informed as required by applicable law.

17. External links and repositories

The Services may contain links to websites, repositories, package registries, or other resources operated by third parties.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies and practices.

18. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Services, our practices, or applicable legal requirements change.

The effective or last-updated date at the top of this page will identify the current version.

Where required by applicable law, users will be informed of material changes by an appropriate notice.
