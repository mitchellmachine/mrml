MRML

An experimental operating system, compiler, and application platform built around owner sovereignty, capability security, and accessibility.

Explore the source ↗ View releases ↗

Designed from first principles

01

Owner sovereignty

The owner—not a vendor, publisher, or remote service—remains the ultimate authority.

02

Explicit authority

Names identify; capabilities authorize. Ambient privilege is replaced by bounded, revocable rights.

03

Accessible by design

Accessibility is part of the architecture, not a compatibility layer added after the fact.
